# Company vision — Nerdy Birdie LLC

Last updated: 27 November 2026 (Gaming Guides repo). Source: founder's "Product Architecture & Ecosystem Alignment" brief, 27 Nov 2026.

## Who we are

**Nerdy Birdie LLC** is the parent company. Its website, https://nerdy-birdie.com, is the corporate portfolio and directory of ventures.
Each venture is its own product with its own site, identity and audience. The corporate site introduces them; it doesn't duplicate them.

## Mission for gaming

Give gamers the tools, knowledge and community resources they need to get more out of the games they love.
We aren't just building gaming guides for gamers. We're building them **with** gamers.

## Priorities, in order

1. **Gaming first.** The product has to be useful in the middle of a play session.
2. **Community second.** COZMC is a connected, optional community that improves the guides and connects players.
3. **Monetization third.** Revenue supports the product; it never blocks ordinary gaming help.

Every major feature and design decision should support these priorities in this order.

## Principles

- No one needs to understand blockchain, cryptocurrency or Web3 to benefit from our gaming products.
- No wallet or Discord account is ever required for ordinary gaming functionality.
- Accuracy over volume: sourced, reviewed information; unverified discoveries never become authoritative automatically.
- Respect third-party intellectual property: our own words, credited sources, no copied guides, maps or art.
- Spoiler-safe by default.
- Build so one product can grow into a library (more games, more platforms) without a redesign.

## Motto

**Brick by Brick, WE Build.**
